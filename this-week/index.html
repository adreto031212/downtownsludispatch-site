This week in Downtown & South Lake Union.

The dated things to know between now and next Thursday, from issue 2 of the Downtown & South Lake Union Dispatch. Every item links its public source.

Heads up

  • Coastal flood watch on the Seattle shoreline, today from 3 AM to 5 PM. The National Weather Service says significant coastal flooding is possible, with up to 2.5 to 3.5 feet of water over low-lying ground along shorelines, and some roads may close. Check the latest before heading near the water, never drive through standing water, and report urgent flooding to Seattle Public Utilities at (206) 386-1800. National Weather Service · SPU
  • Elevators out at Pioneer Square and International District/Chinatown stations. Sound Transit lists elevator 705 at Pioneer Square and elevator 902 at International District/Chinatown out until further notice; the alert page gives the step-free route around each. Sound Transit alerts

What the city decided and what changed

Buses run a Sunday schedule today, and the South Lake Union Streetcar sits out

Metro buses run their Sunday schedule today and a regular weekday schedule Friday.

The city's commercial parking tax goes from 14.5% to 17% on January 1

The Council passed the increase on November 20, the same day it passed the 2027 budget.

The Monorail's fare ceiling rises from $4 to $7

Also on November 20, the Council let Seattle Center amend the Monorail's operating agreement so a one-way adult fare may go as high as $7, and a discounted fare as high as $3.50.

the holiday pickup shift

Seattle Public Utilities does not collect garbage, recycling or food and yard waste today.
