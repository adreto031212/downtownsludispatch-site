The city's commercial parking tax goes from 14.5% to 17% on January 1

November 26, 2026 · Downtown & South Lake Union Dispatch, issue 2 · All Downtown & South Lake Union news

The Council passed the increase on November 20, the same day it passed the 2027 budget. The tax is the parking fee times the rate, and the money goes to transportation. On a $30 day in a downtown garage, the city's share goes from $4.35 to $5.10. Council Bill 121314
