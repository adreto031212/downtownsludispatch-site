Pike Place is open to cars Monday to Thursday this winter, and closes to them on Saturday, November 28

November 19, 2026 · Downtown & South Lake Union Dispatch, issue 1 · All Downtown & South Lake Union news

The Market's winter schedule runs from October 1 to March 31: Monday through Thursday the street is open to traffic, and drivers must yield to people walking; Friday through Sunday, 11 AM to 4 PM, only authorized vehicles may enter. The street is scheduled to close to vehicles for Magic in the Market on November 28 and for Figgy Pudding on Sunday, December 6. Pike Place Market
