Downtown & South Lake Union · One useful thing

the holiday pickup shift

November 26, 2026 · Downtown & South Lake Union Dispatch, issue 2 · All Downtown & South Lake Union news

Seattle Public Utilities does not collect garbage, recycling or food and yard waste today. If your pickup day is Thursday, it moves to Friday, November 27; Monday to Wednesday pickups this week are unchanged. The North and South Transfer Stations are closed today and reopen Friday. The same one-day shift comes back for Christmas (Friday, December 25, moves to Saturday) and New Year's Day (Friday, January 1, moves to Saturday, January 2). Seattle Public Utilities
