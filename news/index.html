What changed in Downtown & South Lake Union.

Every story the Downtown & South Lake Union Dispatch has run, newest first. Each links the public record it came from.

November 26, 2026

Buses run a Sunday schedule today, and the South Lake Union Streetcar sits out

Metro buses run their Sunday schedule today and a regular weekday schedule Friday.

The city's commercial parking tax goes from 14.5% to 17% on January 1

The Council passed the increase on November 20, the same day it passed the 2027 budget.

The Monorail's fare ceiling rises from $4 to $7

Also on November 20, the Council let Seattle Center amend the Monorail's operating agreement so a one-way adult fare may go as high as $7, and a discounted fare as high as $3.50.

the holiday pickup shift

Seattle Public Utilities does not collect garbage, recycling or food and yard waste today.

November 19, 2026

Westlake Park lights its holiday tree Friday, November 27, at 5 PM, with fireworks right after

The Downtown Seattle Association's free celebration starts at 11 AM in the park, with crafts and costumed characters before the lighting.

Pike Place is open to cars Monday to Thursday this winter, and closes to them on Saturday, November 28

The Market's winter schedule runs from October 1 to March 31: Monday through Thursday the street is open to traffic, and drivers must yield to people walking; Friday through Sunday, 11 AM to 4 PM…

A 271-seat chamber music hall has its permit at Sixth and Union

On October 19 the city issued the permit to change part of 1405 Sixth Avenue from medical services to a concert hall for the Seattle Chamber Music Society, $4.38 million of interior work in suite 305.

food scraps in a small kitchen

In Seattle, food waste and recyclables are not allowed in the garbage (SMC 21.36.082-083), and that holds in apartments and condos too.
